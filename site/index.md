# UPSC GS Mains — revision notes

Notes and handwritten answers for UPSC CSE Mains **GS1–GS3**, written in public between October 2026 and
February 2027. Each topic page has the notes, followed by the past Mains questions on that topic with a scan
of a timed, handwritten answer.

- **[Revise today](revise-today.md)**: topics due for spaced revision (1, 7, 30 and 90 days after merge).
- **[Progress](progress.md)**: topics done per paper, milestones and what's overdue.

Question text is reproduced from the official UPSC papers for non-commercial study; see the
[repository](https://github.com/nononsenseupsctamil/upsc-gs-mains) for attribution. No question text here is
AI-generated.
